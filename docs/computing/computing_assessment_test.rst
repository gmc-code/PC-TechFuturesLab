==============================================
Assessment: computing and cybersecurity test
==============================================

.. list-table::
   :widths: 30 70

   * - **Type**
     - Online multiple-choice test
   * - **Marks**
     - 20
   * - **Dates**
     - Opens Term 1, Week 1. Due Term 1, Week 3.
   * - **Duration**
     - Completed during Weeks 1–3
   * - **Achievement standard**
     - Students investigate the role of hardware and software in managing, controlling and securing the
       movement of and access to data in networked digital systems.

Learning intentions
--------------------

* I can show what I know about hardware, software, data and cybersecurity.
* I can apply what I know to new situations, such as spotting a phishing message I have not seen before.

How to prepare
---------------

Stage 1: Learn (Weeks 1–3)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* Complete the activities on each lesson page.
* Work through the Grok Academy course
  `Information privacy and security <https://groklearning.com/course/cyber-hs-infosec-v2/>`_ and take
  summary notes of each module.

Stage 2: Revise (Week 3)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* Use the flashcards, crossword and practice questions on the :doc:`computing_revision` page.
* Do the practice test below until you can score 16 or more.
* Write down any questions you got wrong and look up the answer in the lesson pages.

Stage 3: Sit the test
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* Your teacher will give you the link to the online test.
* Read every option before choosing. Some questions ask for **two** answers.
* You can go back and change answers before you submit.

What to submit
---------------

* The completed online test (submitted through the link your teacher gives you).
* Your Grok summary notes, if your teacher asks for them.

Practice test
--------------

This practice test is like the real test. It picks 10 questions at random each time you press
**Reset Group**.

.. mcqgroup::
   :shuffle-questions:
   :num-questions: 10
   :show-instant-feedback:

   .. multichoice::

      Which part of the computer carries out the instructions in a program?

      [x] CPU | Correct.
      [ ] SSD | Storage keeps data.
      [ ] Monitor | Output device.
      [ ] Router | Communication device.

   .. multichoice::

      A microphone is an example of which type of hardware?

      [x] Input | Correct. It gets sound into the computer.
      [ ] Output | Speakers are output.
      [ ] Storage | It does not keep data.
      [ ] Processing | It does not process instructions.

   .. multichoice::

      Which TWO are examples of system software?

      [x] Windows 11 | Correct. An operating system.
      [x] A printer driver | Correct. Drivers let the OS talk to hardware.
      [ ] Photoshop | Application software.
      [ ] Minecraft | Application software.

   .. multichoice::

      What does encryption do?

      [ ] Makes files smaller | That is compression.
      [x] Scrambles data so only someone with the key can read it | Correct.
      [ ] Deletes viruses | That is antivirus software.
      [ ] Speeds up the internet | It does not change speed.

   .. multichoice::

      A list of every temperature reading from a weather station is:

      [x] data | Correct. Raw facts.
      [ ] information | It has not been processed yet.
      [ ] software | Not a program.
      [ ] a firewall | Not a security system.

   .. multichoice::

      Which is NOT one of the four parts of an information system?

      [ ] People | Part of the system.
      [ ] Data | Part of the system.
      [ ] Processes | Part of the system.
      [x] Advertising | Correct. The fourth part is digital systems.

   .. multichoice::

      Which post gives away the MOST personal information?

      [ ] "Loved the new superhero movie!" | An opinion only.
      [ ] A drawing of a dragon | No personal details.
      [x] A selfie in school uniform tagged at your street | Correct. Shows your school and where you live.
      [ ] "Go Tigers!" | A team you support.

   .. multichoice::

      Using someone else's song in your video without permission may break the:

      [x] Copyright Act | Correct.
      [ ] Privacy Act | The Privacy Act is about personal information.
      [ ] Social Media Minimum Age law | That law is about account ages.
      [ ] Road Rules | Not related.

   .. multichoice::

      The ACSC recommends a passphrase of at least:

      [ ] 2 words and 8 characters | Too short.
      [x] 4 random words and 15 characters | Correct.
      [ ] 1 word with symbols | Predictable.
      [ ] 6 numbers | A PIN, not a passphrase.

   .. multichoice::

      Why should you use a different passphrase for every account?

      [ ] It is required by law | It is advice, not law.
      [x] If one website is hacked, your other accounts stay safe | Correct. This stops credential stuffing.
      [ ] It makes websites load faster | No effect on speed.
      [ ] So you can share one with friends | Never share passwords.

   .. multichoice::

      A password and a PIN together count as multi-factor authentication.

      [ ] True | Both are something you know.
      [x] False | Correct. MFA needs different types of factor.

   .. multichoice::

      An authenticator app on your phone is which type of factor?

      [ ] Something you know | You do not memorise the code.
      [x] Something you have | Correct. You need the phone.
      [ ] Something you are | Not a biometric.
      [ ] Not a factor | It is a factor.

   .. multichoice::

      A text message asks you to pay a $2 parcel fee within 12 hours. This is most likely:

      [x] smishing | Correct. A phishing text using urgency.
      [ ] a software update | Updates do not ask for payment by text.
      [ ] a firewall alert | Not related.
      [ ] two-factor authentication | Not a login.

   .. multichoice::

      Where should you report a scam in Australia?

      [x] Scamwatch | Correct.
      [ ] The app store | Not the right place.
      [ ] A social media comment | Not the right place.
      [ ] Nowhere, just delete it | Reporting helps warn others.

   .. multichoice::

      Malware that is disguised as a free game hack is a:

      [ ] virus | A virus spreads by attaching to files.
      [x] trojan | Correct.
      [ ] firewall | A firewall protects networks.
      [ ] passphrase | Not malware.

Exam-style question
--------------------

.. structuredquestion:: Securing a home network
   :total-marks: 5
   :category: Test preparation

   .. stimulus::

      The Nguyen family has a laptop, two phones and a games console connected to their home Wi-Fi router.
      Everyone uses the same password, ``nguyen123``, for all their accounts.

   .. subquestion:: a) Explain how hardware and software on the router help secure the home network.
      :marks: 2

      .. model-answer::

         The router has a built-in firewall that checks data coming into the network and blocks anything not
         allowed. It also uses a Wi-Fi password and encryption so only people with the password can join the
         network and read the data.

      .. marking-guidance::

         * 1 mark for a security feature (firewall, Wi-Fi password, encryption).
         * 1 mark for explaining how it protects the network.

   .. subquestion:: b) Recommend three changes the family should make to improve their security.
      :marks: 3

      .. model-answer::

         * Use a different passphrase for each person and each account (four or more random words, at least
           15 characters), stored in a password manager.
         * Turn on multi-factor authentication for important accounts such as email and banking.
         * Turn on automatic updates for all devices and the router.

      .. marking-guidance::

         1 mark for each sensible, specific recommendation (maximum 3).
