Summary
--------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 30 70

   * - Lesson
     - Key ideas
   * - :doc:`computing_01_why_computing`
     - Computers take in data, process it, store it and give out results. A program is a set of
       instructions. There are many IT careers and pathways.
   * - :doc:`computing_02_hardware_software`
     - Hardware is physical (input, processing, memory, storage, output, communication). Software is
       programs (system and application). Logins, permissions, encryption, firewalls, antivirus,
       updates and backups protect data.
   * - :doc:`computing_03_data_information`
     - Data is raw facts. Information is processed data with meaning. Information systems are made of
       people, data, processes and digital systems.
   * - :doc:`computing_04_sharing_online`
     - Think before you share. Personal information can be misused. Social Media Minimum Age, Online
       Safety Act, Privacy Act, Copyright Act and the Criminal Code.
   * - :doc:`computing_05_passphrases`
     - Passphrases: four or more random words, at least 15 characters, unique for every account. Use a
       password manager.
   * - :doc:`computing_06_mfa`
     - Factors: something you know, have or are. MFA uses two or more **different** types.
   * - :doc:`computing_07_phishing_scams`
     - Phishing warning signs. Stop. Check. Protect. Report to Scamwatch, ReportCyber, IDCARE, eSafety.

Flashcards
-----------

.. flashcards:: Computing and cybersecurity
   :new-per-session: 15
   :shuffle:

   # Hardware and software
   Hardware | The physical parts of a computer that you can touch
   Software | The programs and instructions that run on a computer
   CPU | Central processing unit: carries out the instructions in programs
   RAM | Short-term, volatile memory that holds the programs that are open now
   SSD | Solid state drive: long-term, non-volatile storage
   Operating system | System software that manages the hardware, files and other programs
   Application software | Software that helps the user do a task, such as Photoshop or a browser
   Encryption | Scrambling data so only someone with the key can read it
   Firewall | Hardware or software that blocks data that is not allowed on a network
   # Data and information
   Data | Raw facts and figures
   Information | Data that has been processed so it has meaning
   Information system | People, data, processes and digital systems working together
   Garbage in, garbage out | Wrong data going in gives wrong information coming out
   # Online safety
   Digital footprint | The trail of information you leave online
   Personal information | Information that identifies you or could be used to work out who you are
   Social Media Minimum Age | Age-restricted platforms must stop under-16s having accounts (since 10 December 2025)
   eSafety Commissioner | Australia's online safety regulator, which can order harmful content to be removed
   Copyright | The legal right of a creator to control how their work is copied and shared
   # Accounts
   Passphrase | A password made of four or more random words, at least 15 characters
   Password manager | An app that stores passwords in an encrypted vault
   Credential stuffing | Trying passwords stolen from one website on other websites
   Brute force attack | Trying every possible combination until the password is found
   Multi-factor authentication | Logging in with two or more different types of factor
   Biometrics | Using part of your body, such as a fingerprint or face, to prove who you are
   # Threats
   Phishing | A fake message pretending to be from a trusted organisation to steal details
   Smishing | Phishing by SMS text message
   Ransomware | Malware that locks your files and demands payment
   Trojan | Malware disguised as a useful program
   Stop. Check. Protect. | Scamwatch's three steps for avoiding scams

Practice questions
-------------------

.. mcqgroup::
   :shuffle-questions:

   .. multichoice::

      Which of these is a **communication** device?

      [ ] Monitor | A monitor is an output device.
      [ ] SSD | An SSD is storage.
      [x] Router | Correct. A router sends and receives data between networks.
      [ ] Keyboard | A keyboard is an input device.

   .. multichoice::

      Which statement about RAM is correct?

      [x] It is volatile | Correct. It loses its data when the power is off.
      [ ] It keeps files when the computer is off | That describes storage.
      [ ] It is the same as the CPU | The CPU processes. RAM holds data being used.
      [ ] It is a type of software | RAM is hardware.

   .. multichoice::

      "The average score was 74%" is an example of:

      [ ] data | It has been processed, so it is information.
      [x] information | Correct. The marks have been processed into an average.
      [ ] hardware | Not a physical part.
      [ ] a program | Not a set of instructions.

   .. multichoice::

      Which is the strongest passphrase?

      [ ] Fluffy2010! | Pet name and year.
      [ ] i love pizza | A predictable sentence, only 12 characters.
      [x] dolphin ladder copper mitten | Correct. Four random words and more than 15 characters.
      [ ] qwerty123 | A keyboard pattern.

   .. multichoice::

      A fingerprint is which type of factor?

      [ ] Something you know | That would be a password or PIN.
      [ ] Something you have | That would be a phone or key.
      [x] Something you are | Correct. Biometrics are something you are.
      [ ] It is not a factor | It is a biometric factor.

   .. multichoice::

      Since 10 December 2025, who must take reasonable steps to stop under-16s from having accounts on
      age-restricted social media?

      [ ] Parents | The responsibility is not on parents.
      [ ] Schools | Schools do not run these platforms.
      [x] The social media platforms | Correct. The law puts the responsibility on the platforms.
      [ ] Young people themselves | Young people are not fined under this law.

   .. multichoice::

      What should you do if you receive a text from your "bank" with a link to verify your account?

      [ ] Click the link quickly before the deadline | Urgency is a warning sign.
      [ ] Reply with your account number | Never send your details.
      [x] Open your bank's official app or call the number on your card to check | Correct. Check using contact details you find yourself.
      [ ] Forward it to your friends to warn them | Better to report it to Scamwatch.

   .. multichoice::

      Ransomware is best protected against by:

      [ ] using a bigger monitor | Not related.
      [x] keeping regular backups and updating software | Correct. Backups mean you can restore your files without paying.
      [ ] using a shorter password | Shorter passwords are weaker.
      [ ] turning off the firewall | That makes things worse.

Exam-style question
--------------------

.. structuredquestion:: Topic review
   :total-marks: 6
   :category: Revision

   .. stimulus::

      Lucy's family runs a small bakery. They use a laptop to take online orders and store customer names,
      phone numbers and addresses. One morning a pop-up appears: "Your files have been encrypted. Pay $500
      in cryptocurrency to get them back."

   .. subquestion:: a) Identify the type of malware and describe what it has done.
      :marks: 2

      .. model-answer::

         Ransomware. It has encrypted (locked) the bakery's files so they cannot be opened, and it is
         demanding a payment to unlock them.

      .. marking-guidance::

         * 1 mark for ransomware.
         * 1 mark for describing that files are encrypted or locked and payment is demanded.

   .. subquestion:: b) Explain why the bakery has a responsibility to protect its customers' information.
      :marks: 2

      .. model-answer::

         The customer names, phone numbers and addresses are personal information. Customers trust the bakery
         to keep it secure, and privacy law (the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act) expects
         organisations it covers to protect personal information. If it is stolen, customers could be
         scammed.

      .. marking-guidance::

         * 1 mark for recognising the data is personal information.
         * 1 mark for a reason (privacy law, trust or risk of harm to customers).

   .. subquestion:: c) Describe two things the bakery could have done to reduce the damage from this attack.
      :marks: 2

      .. model-answer::

         * Kept regular backups on a separate drive or in the cloud, so files could be restored without paying.
         * Kept the operating system and antivirus software updated so security holes were fixed.
         * Trained staff not to open suspicious email attachments.

      .. marking-guidance::

         1 mark for each sensible protection (maximum 2).
