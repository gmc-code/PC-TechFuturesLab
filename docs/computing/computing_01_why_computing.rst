====================================
1. Why computing matters
====================================

Learning intentions
--------------------

* I can explain why computing and programming are important in everyday life.
* I can describe what computer programming is.
* I can name some jobs in information technology (IT) and the study pathways that lead to them.

Success criteria
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* I can give three examples of computers helping people in daily life.
* I can explain the words **program**, **programmer** and **programming language**.
* I can research one IT job and describe what the job involves and how to get there.

Computers are everywhere
-------------------------

A **computer** is any device that takes in data, processes it, stores it and gives out a result.
That means a lot more than laptops. Your phone, a smartwatch, a supermarket checkout, a car's
braking system and a traffic light controller are all computers.

Think about a normal school day:

* Your phone alarm wakes you up. *(A computer kept the time and played a sound.)*
* You tap your card or phone on the bus. *(A computer checked your ticket and took the fare.)*
* You check your timetable on the school portal. *(Computers stored, found and showed your timetable.)*
* You message a friend. *(Computers sent your message across a network in less than a second.)*

Computing matters because it helps people **communicate**, **solve problems**, **save time** and
**do things that would be impossible by hand**, such as forecasting the weather or scanning
the inside of a body.

Worked example: one task, many computers
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Task:** Ordering food on an app.

#. The **app** on your phone shows a menu (software running on hardware).
#. You tap your order. The app sends it over the **internet** to the restaurant's computer.
#. A **payment system** checks your bank card and approves the payment.
#. The restaurant's screen shows the order to the cooks.
#. A **map system** uses GPS to show you where the driver is.

At least five different computer systems worked together just to get you a burger.

What is computer programming?
------------------------------

A computer cannot think for itself. It follows **instructions**.

* A **program** is a set of instructions that tells a computer what to do.
* **Programming** (or coding) is writing those instructions.
* A **programmer** is a person who writes programs.
* A **programming language** is a special language that both people and computers can understand,
  for example **Python**, JavaScript or Scratch.

Here is a tiny Python program. It gives the computer two instructions.

.. code-block:: python

   print("Hello!")
   print("Welcome to TechFuturesLab.")

Output:

.. code-block:: text

   Hello!
   Welcome to TechFuturesLab.

The computer does **exactly** what the program says, in order, from top to bottom. If an
instruction is wrong, the computer will still follow it, and you get the wrong result. Finding
and fixing these mistakes is called **debugging**.

.. admonition:: Why learn to program?
   :class: tip

   Even if you never become a programmer, learning to code teaches you to break big problems into
   small steps, to test your ideas and to keep trying when something does not work the first time.
   These skills help in every subject and every job.

Jobs in IT
-----------

Almost every industry now needs people with digital skills. Here are some IT jobs.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 25 75

   * - Job
     - What they do
   * - Software developer
     - Designs, writes and tests programs and apps.
   * - Cybersecurity analyst
     - Protects computer systems and data from attacks.
   * - Data scientist
     - Collects and studies large amounts of data to find patterns and answer questions.
   * - Game developer
     - Designs and programs video games.
   * - UX/UI designer
     - Designs how apps and websites look and how easy they are to use.
   * - Network engineer
     - Sets up and looks after the networks that connect computers.
   * - AR/VR developer
     - Builds augmented and virtual reality experiences for games, training and education.
   * - AI engineer
     - Builds and trains artificial intelligence systems.
   * - IT support technician
     - Helps people fix problems with their computers, devices and accounts.

Pathways
~~~~~~~~~

There is more than one way into an IT career.

* **School:** Digital Technologies, Software Development, Mathematics and VCE subjects such as
  Applied Computing.
* **TAFE:** Certificates and diplomas in IT, cybersecurity and programming.
* **University:** Degrees such as Bachelor of Computer Science, Information Technology,
  Software Engineering, Cyber Security or Data Science.
* **On the job:** Traineeships, apprenticeships and industry certificates.

Activity 3: Check your understanding
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. mcqgroup::

   .. multichoice::

      Which of these is the best description of a computer?

      [ ] A machine with a keyboard and a screen | Many computers, such as smartwatches and traffic lights, have no keyboard.
      [x] A device that takes in data, processes it, stores it and gives out a result | Correct. This describes every type of computer.
      [ ] Any machine that uses electricity | A toaster uses electricity but does not process data.
      [ ] A device that can connect to the internet | Many computers work without the internet.

   .. multichoice::

      What happens if a program has a wrong instruction in it?

      [ ] The computer fixes it automatically | Computers cannot guess what you meant.
      [x] The computer follows the wrong instruction anyway | Correct. The computer does exactly what it is told.
      [ ] The computer skips that instruction | It does not skip it. It runs it or stops with an error.
      [ ] The computer asks the programmer what they meant | Computers cannot ask what you meant.

   .. multichoice::

      [ ] UX/UI designer | UX/UI designers focus on how easy apps are to use.
      [x] Cybersecurity analyst | Correct. Cybersecurity analysts protect systems and data.
      [ ] Data scientist | Data scientists find patterns in data.

Exam-style question
--------------------

.. structuredquestion:: Computers in everyday life
   :total-marks: 6
   :category: Introduction to computing

   .. stimulus::

      Sam uses a fitness app on a smartwatch. The watch counts Sam's steps and measures heart rate.
      Each night, the watch sends the data to an app on Sam's phone, which shows a graph of the week.

   .. subquestion:: a) Explain why the smartwatch is a computer.
      :marks: 2

      Use the definition of a computer in your answer.

      .. model-answer::

         A computer takes in data, processes it, stores it and gives out a result. The smartwatch
         takes in data (steps and heart rate), processes and stores it, and gives out results such as
         the step count on the screen.

      .. marking-guidance::

         * 1 mark for giving a correct definition of a computer (input, process, store, output).
         * 1 mark for linking the definition to the smartwatch with an example.

   .. subquestion:: b) Define the term program.
      :marks: 1

      .. model-answer::

         A program is a set of instructions that tells a computer what to do.

      .. marking-guidance::

         1 mark for "set of instructions" (or similar) for a computer.

   .. subquestion:: c) Name one IT job that would be needed to create the fitness app and describe what that person does.
      :marks: 2

      .. model-answer::

         A software developer would design, write and test the code for the app.
         (Other answers: a UX/UI designer designs how the app looks and how easy it is to use;
         a data scientist works out how to turn the data into useful graphs.)

      .. marking-guidance::

         * 1 mark for a suitable IT job.
         * 1 mark for a correct description of what that job does for this app.

   .. subquestion:: d) Give one way the fitness app helps Sam.
      :marks: 1

      .. model-answer::

         It shows Sam a graph of steps and heart rate for the week, so Sam can see how active
         they have been and set goals.

      .. marking-guidance::

         1 mark for any reasonable benefit linked to the app's data.
